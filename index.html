FAQ

Questions before we start?

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

What does “custom software” mean?

Software designed and built from scratch for your business: your processes, your users and your data. Instead of adapting your team to a generic tool, the tool adapts to you.

How long does a project take?

A website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks and a web platform 6 to 12 weeks. After the discovery phase you get a detailed timeline with fixed milestones.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the scope. After a first call we send a clear quote with a fixed price per phase, so you always know what you are paying for.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The code, the data and the accounts are yours from day one. You can keep working with us or take the project anywhere.

Can you integrate with the systems we already use?

Yes. We connect with ERPs, payment gateways, messaging, accounting and any service with an API — or build one when it doesn’t exist.

What technologies do you work with?

Modern, proven web technology: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL and major cloud providers. We choose the stack that best fits each project.

What happens after launch?

We monitor the platform, run backups, fix issues and keep improving it with a monthly care plan that fits your needs.

Can you improve an existing platform?

Yes. We audit what you have, fix what is urgent and plan improvements step by step — without stopping your operation.
